# Taste

## Workflow
- Wants edits strictly scoped to what was asked: when they say "do not change anything without it" / "do not change anything else", only touch the named classes/elements and leave everything else (other classes on the same element, other components) byte-for-byte intact. Confidence: 0.7
- When asking how to fix or restyle something, wants step-by-step instructions (including the diagnosis and proposed solution) rather than the assistant editing files directly; often says "do not change anything in this code" and expects code shown as illustrative snippets only. Confidence: 0.95
